NexaPDF

Your Android PDF workspace. Read, organize, convert, compress, scan, annotate and protect documents with a local-first workflow.

Designed around your documents

NexaPDF is being built to keep core PDF operations on your device. You select the files to work with and choose where to save the result. Some optional tools, such as the Google ML Kit document scanner, depend on platform services.
